AI Safety & Data Hygiene

Using AI tools safely at work and protecting sensitive information.

Golden Rule: Use Official Tools Only

Stick to official platforms with corporate accounts and data agreements:

  • ChatGPT (openai.com, ChatGPT Plus, or enterprise)
  • Claude (claude.ai or Anthropic API with enterprise agreements)
  • Gemini (Google account, or Google Cloud setup)
  • Your company's approved AI platform or managed instance

Avoid random free sites, unverified alternatives, or tools without clear data policies. Free tools often train on your inputs.

Never paste data into random AI websites. You have no idea where it goes or how long it's stored.

What You Can Share

Safe to share with AI:

  • Public code (repos you'd push to GitHub)
  • General technical questions and debugging
  • Non-confidential documentation and specs
  • Stack traces and error messages (without secrets)
  • System architecture diagrams
  • Sanitized code samples for learning

What You CANNOT Share

Never paste into any AI tool:

  • API keys, tokens, credentials, passwords, SSH keys
  • Customer data, user information, email addresses, phone numbers
  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII: SSN, birthdate, address)
  • Proprietary code, unpublished algorithms, or trade secrets (unless approved by legal)
  • Private keys, certificates, or encryption material
  • Actual database credentials or connection strings
  • Health records, financial information, or sensitive personal data

If a secret appears in your error log or code snippet, revoke it immediately. Assume it's compromised.

Data Privacy Best Practices

  • Anonymize: Replace real names with "User123", real URLs with "example.com", real numbers with dummy values
  • Redact: Use grep to filter secrets before pasting: `grep -v "PRIVATE_KEY\|PASSWORD\|TOKEN"` before copy-pasting
  • Check your prompt: Before hitting send, ask: "Does this contain anything I wouldn't share in a public Slack channel?"
  • Use official channels: Company-approved tools often have data residency and retention controls

Pro tip: Create a sanitized version of your code or data specifically for pasting into AI tools. Use template values like YOUR_API_KEY_HERE.

Opt Out of Data Collection

Many AI platforms offer opt-outs to prevent your data from being used for training:

  • OpenAI: Disable chat history in settings. With enterprise accounts, data is never used for training.
  • Anthropic: Check data processing agreements. Enterprise plans offer data residency.
  • Google Gemini: Disable activity logging in Google Account settings.

If your company has an enterprise account, data handling is usually contractually guaranteed. Check with your admin.

Know Your Company's AI Policy

Most companies in 2026 now have formal AI acceptable-use policies. Find yours and follow it:

  • Which tools are approved? Some companies restrict to Claude or GPT only.
  • What data is forbidden? Customer data, proprietary info, PII, etc.
  • Do I need approval for certain use cases? Some teams require review of code sent to AI.
  • What's the data residency requirement? Some jurisdictions have rules about where data is stored.

If your company doesn't have an AI policy, that's a security risk. Escalate to your manager or security team.

Red Flags & Quick Checklist

Don't use this tool if:

  • You're not sure who owns the data (is it a startup? A random site?)
  • The privacy policy says "we use your data for training"
  • There's no data deletion policy or GDPR compliance
  • Your company explicitly forbids it
  • You're handling healthcare, financial, or government data

Before pasting anything:

  • [ ] Does this contain secrets or credentials?
  • [ ] Does this have customer or user data?
  • [ ] Could this reveal proprietary information?
  • [ ] Is this tool on my company's approved list?
